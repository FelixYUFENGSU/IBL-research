The mouse in this experiment had thermistors implanted between the nasal bone and inner nasal epithelium as "true" breathing. 

**Key Differences** 
- Recording devices in the LFP study are placed on the left olfactory bulb about 1mm deep. Our LFP is in an epidural array on the surface. 
**Their results**

OB(Olfactory bulb) LFPs across multiple frequency bands align with respiratory cycles. These OB rhythms are locked to time in the respiratory cycle and not phase. 
2-12Hz LFP effectively track sniffing rate. 


**Their methods** 

Data preprocessing: lowpass filtering the singal below 300Hz and used an order 4 Butterworth filter, followed by downsampling to 1kHz
